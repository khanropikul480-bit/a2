Terms of Bespoke Commission & Salon Protocol

Standard operating conditions governing custom commissions, salon fittings, and artisanal creation.

8. Intellectual Property and Bespoke Design Ownership

All original garment patterns, technical drafting blocks, structural canvas sketches, and bespoke silhouette architectures developed by VelvetGarbQuay remain the exclusive intellectual and creative property of the Maison. The Patron acquires the physical bespoke garment and the right to wear the creation publicly and privately in perpetuity.

However, the Patron does not acquire commercial reproduction rights, manufacturing licenses, or authorization to clone or mass-produce the proprietary pattern block for commercial distribution. High-resolution photographic imagery of bespoke garments captured within our salons remains subject to mutual editorial copyright agreements.

9. Salon Etiquette and Appointment Cancellation Policies

To maintain the serene, unhurried atmosphere of our private salons, appointments are scheduled with generous buffer windows between patrons. We kindly request that patrons provide a minimum of forty-eight hours' advance notice for any appointment rescheduling or cancellation.

Appointments cancelled with less than twenty-four hours' notice may be subject to a salon rebooking reservation fee, which is credited in full toward subsequent bespoke commissions. Patrons are accompanied by their designated master cutter throughout their consultation, ensuring complete privacy, personal security, and undivided attention.

10. Archival Storage and Unclaimed Commissions

Upon completion of the final hand pressing and quality inspection, the Maison notifies the Patron via secure email and direct telephone concierge. Patrons are requested to schedule their final collection or courier dispatch within thirty calendar days of completion notification.

Finished bespoke creations unclaimed after ninety calendar days are transferred to our archival conservation vault. Due to the specialized climate control requirements and dedicated vault insurance, unclaimed garments stored beyond ninety days may incur a nominal monthly conservation fee. Garments stored beyond twelve calendar months remain the property of the patron, with the Maison reserving the right to seek settlement of accrued storage and insurance costs before release.

1. Scope of Agreement and Commission Acceptance

These Terms of Bespoke Commission ("Terms") govern all custom tailoring services, haute couture creations, salon consultations, and textile procurement provided by VelvetGarbQuay ("the Maison") to the commissioning client ("the Patron"). By submitting a bespoke commission order, signing a design proposal, or remitting an initial textile deposit, the Patron agrees to be bound by these Terms in full.

Every bespoke garment crafted by VelvetGarbQuay is an individual work of artisanal couture tailored exclusively to the unique anatomical measurements, posture, and stylistic preferences of the Patron. Consequently, standard retail return frameworks and cooling-off cancellation rights for off-the-rack consumer goods do not apply to custom bespoke commissions.

2. Commission Deposit & Payment Structure

Bespoke commissions are executed under a structured payment schedule designed to fund specialized textile procurement and dedicated master artisan labor:

  • Initial Commitment Deposit (50%): Due upon approval of the design blueprint and fabric selection. This non-refundable deposit secures the required yardage of pure silk velvet, floating horsehair canvas, and specialty linings from European mills, and initiates paper block drafting.
  • Interim Fitting Balance (25%): Due upon completion of the second in-person fitting (skeleton canvas stage).
  • Final Delivery Balance (25%): Due upon final inspection and acceptance of the finished garment prior to salon collection or white-glove courier dispatch.

Invoices must be settled within fourteen calendar days of issuance. Late payments may result in the rescheduling of dedicated master tailor workbench hours and adjustment of estimated completion delivery timelines.

3. The Fitting Protocol & Patron Responsibilities

The precision of bespoke tailoring depends upon collaborative engagement during scheduled in-person fittings. A standard commission requires between three and four fittings:

  • Muslin Toile Fitting: Evaluation of overall proportions, stance balance, and initial pattern drape using unbleached cotton muslin.
  • Skeleton Canvas Fitting: Assessment of the basted velvet shell structured with the floating horsehair chest canvas.
  • Finishing & Balance Fitting: Verification of sleeve pitch, hem roll, button placement, and collar closeness.

Patrons are expected to attend fittings wearing the specific undergarments, footwear, and heel heights intended to be worn with the finished garment. Substantial anatomical fluctuations (exceeding +/- 5% body mass) occurring after muslin approval may necessitate pattern re-drafting and incur additional labor charges.

4. Textile Characteristics and Natural Variations

VelvetGarbQuay works exclusively with 100% organic Mulberry silk velvets, heritage British wools, and handcrafted natural textiles. Patrons acknowledge that natural fibers possess organic irregularities, including subtle variations in pile texture, directional light sheen, and artisanal dye depth across continuous fabric bolts. These characteristics are hallmarks of authentic bespoke luxury and do not constitute manufacturing defects.

5. Creation Timelines and Unforeseen Delays

Standard bespoke commissions require an estimated four to six weeks from initial pattern drafting to final hand pressing. While the Maison makes every reasonable effort to honor agreed delivery dates, couture craftsmanship cannot be compromised by artificial acceleration. The Maison shall not be held liable for delays arising from specialized European mill weaving queues, custom botanical dye baths, or international customs clearances beyond our direct control.

6. Post-Delivery Adjustments & Lifetime Craft Warranty

VelvetGarbQuay stands behind the structural integrity of its garments. All bespoke commissions include complimentary minor fitting adjustments within 60 days of final collection, provided the garment has been worn in accordance with our care protocols. Alterations requested as a result of subsequent lifestyle changes or significant weight fluctuation beyond 60 days will be accommodated under standard atelier alteration rates.

7. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of France for commissions initiated in Paris, the laws of England and Wales for commissions in London, and the laws of the State of New York for commissions in New York. Any disputes arising from a bespoke commission shall be resolved through amicable private salon mediation before pursuing formal commercial arbitration.
