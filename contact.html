The Anatomical Measurement Experience

During your private salon consultation, your master tailor records thirty-two distinct anatomical measurements. These encompass cervical spine incline, shoulder slope angle, thoracic chest expansion, waist-to-hip ratio, bicep pitch, and walking stride clearance.

This meticulous data allows us to draft an individual paper block that accounts for your natural breathing rhythm and kinetic movement. We invite you to schedule your appointment at your earliest convenience to begin your bespoke sartorial journey.

Bespoke Commission Timeline and Preparation

To ensure an effortless consultation experience, we encourage patrons to allow approximately ninety minutes for their initial anatomical measurement session. Our senior cutters will guide you through our comprehensive textile library, showcasing over two hundred rare silk velvet swatches, English wools, and specialty silk satin linings.

For eveningwear and black-tie commissions intended for specific gala dates, anniversary celebrations, or international opera openings, we recommend initiating consultations six to eight weeks in advance to accommodate dedicated hand-basting and multiple muslin toile fittings without time compression.

Bespoke Wardrobe Insurance & Valuation

Salon Experience

The Private Consultation Protocol

Every bespoke commission at VelvetGarbQuay begins with an exclusive private salon consultation. During your 90-minute appointment, you will meet directly with a master cutter to explore rare silk velvet bolts, examine floating horsehair canvas samples, discuss garment proportions, and establish your personal 32-point anatomical profile.

Our salon appointments are strictly private, ensuring complete discretion and undivided attention. Patrons are invited to bring inspiration materials, heirloom jewelry, or footwear intended to accompany the finished creation.
