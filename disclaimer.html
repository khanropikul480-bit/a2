5. Historical and Technical Research Representations

The historical narratives, guild regulations, and textile terminology presented across VelvetGarbQuay publications—including references to Venetian sumptuary laws, Lyonnais double-cloth looms, and traditional Sericulture guilds—are compiled through rigorous historical research and archival consultation.

7. Environmental and Biological Fiber Disclosures

VelvetGarbQuay crafts bespoke garments exclusively from 100% natural organic protein and cellulose fibers, including Mulberry silk, Scottish Highland wools, Mongolian horsehair canvas, and Irish linen. Because these materials are biologically pure and processed without synthetic chemical coatings, they are naturally susceptible to ambient environmental factors.

Exposure to direct ultraviolet solar radiation over prolonged periods can cause natural botanical dye pigments to undergo subtle, organic chromatic softening. Similarly, high ambient humidity may temporarily relax silk pile fibers. These physical behaviors are natural characteristics of living biological textiles and do not represent material defects or craftsmanship failures.

2. Digital Display Colorimetry & Textile Perception

Silk velvet is an anisotropic optical textile whose appearance varies dynamically depending on ambient light angles, color temperature, and viewer perspective. Digital photographs and lookbook images displayed across computer monitors, tablets, and smartphone displays are subject to individual screen calibration, color gamuts (sRGB vs. DCI-P3), and ambient display backlighting.

Consequently, digital previews of fabric shades—including our signature midnight burgundies, imperial indigos, and champagne golds—may differ subtly from physical fabric bolts examined under natural daylight or incandescent evening chandelier illumination. We strongly encourage all commissioning patrons to inspect physical textile swatches during their private salon consultation before finalizing dye choices.

3. Garment Care and Preservation Disclaimers

Our published garment care instructions, including vertical steam inversion protocols, horsehair brushing techniques, and cedar storage recommendations, represent standard best practices within haute couture preservation. However, individual fabric responses can vary based on regional humidity, ambient water mineral content, and prior wear conditions. VelvetGarbQuay accepts no liability for garment damage resulting from improper home laundering, direct flat iron contact, synthetic aerosol exposure, or unauthorized third-party dry cleaning treatments.
