Culinary & Allergen Disclaimer

Transparent standards and ethical customer care at Saffron Nibble.

The information presented on https://saffronnibble.com is provided for culinary appreciation, educational insight into saffron botany, and gastronomic enjoyment.

Food Allergen Notice

Our confectionery workshop at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States crafts artisanal foods in an active culinary environment. Our confections regularly incorporate dairy (butter, milk), eggs, wheat flour (gluten), and tree nuts (including Persian pistachios, Italian almonds, and walnuts).

While we observe stringent sanitization protocols between baking runs, trace cross-contact with tree nuts, wheat, or dairy can occur. Customers with severe anaphylactic allergies must exercise informed caution before consuming our delicacies.

Botanical Saffron Information

Culinary saffron is a revered spice celebrated for centuries across Mediterranean and Asian cuisines. Information regarding historical herbal traditions or botanical compounds (crocin, safranal, picrocrocin) is provided purely for cultural and culinary enrichment. It is not intended as medical advice, diagnosis, or health treatment.
