Privacy Policy

Transparent standards and ethical customer care at Saffron Nibble.

At Saffron Nibble ("we", "our", "us"), accessible from https://saffronnibble.com, the privacy of our visitors and culinary clientele is paramount. This Privacy Policy document describes the types of information that is collected and recorded by Saffron Nibble and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you place an order for our artisanal confectionery tasting flights, reserve a private atelier tasting, or contact us via our website forms, we collect personal information you provide voluntarily. This may include your full name, delivery address, telephone number, billing address, and electronic mail address.

We do not store complete payment card credentials on our servers; transactions are processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways.

How We Utilize Your Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly to fulfill orders, coordinate white-glove courier deliveries, send order status updates, and respond to custom corporate gifting inquiries. With your explicit consent, we may occasionally share announcements regarding seasonal saffron harvest releases.

Log Files and Analytical Telemetry

Saffron Nibble follows standard procedures using log files. These files record visitors when they navigate our web pages. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser classification, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click paths.

Third-Party Privacy Practices

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or third-party web entities. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party servers for detailed information on their practices and opt-out directives.

Data Protection Rights

Under applicable global data privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you possess the right to request access, rectification, erasure, or portability of your personal customer records held in our atelier archives. To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy compliance team at atelier@saffronnibble.com or visit us at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
