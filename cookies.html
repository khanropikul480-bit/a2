5. Browser-Specific Cookie Management Protocols

Patrons seeking granular control over stored cookies can configure their web browser settings directly. Below are standard management pathways for major desktop and mobile browsers:

  • Apple Safari: Navigate to Settings > Safari > Privacy & Security > Block All Cookies or Prevent Cross-Site Tracking.
  • Google Chrome: Navigate to Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data > Choose preferred retention parameters.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Navigate to Options > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection > Custom Controls.
  • Microsoft Edge: Navigate to Settings > Site Permissions > Cookies and site data > Manage and delete stored tokens.

6. Updates to Our Digital Cookie Governance

VelvetGarbQuay reserves the right to update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect emerging digital security standards and international privacy mandates. All modifications take effect immediately upon publication on our digital atelier. Patrons are encouraged to review this governance page periodically to stay informed of our digital stewardship practices.

7. Dedicated Privacy and Token Controls

We empower our patrons with complete sovereignty over their digital footprint. If you choose to clear your browser cache and stored session tokens at the conclusion of your visit, your visual theme preference will simply reset to our default warm parchment light aesthetic upon your next visit.

For patrons utilizing privacy-focused browsers such as Brave, DuckDuckGo, or Tor, our digital atelier remains fully functional. We do not employ intrusive browser fingerprinting techniques or circumvent client-side ad-blocking mechanisms. For further inquiries regarding our digital token governance, please contact our Data Protection Concierge at privacy@velvetgarbquay.com.

8. Contacting Our Data Ethics Committee

Should you require formal documentation regarding our data handling protocols or wish to request a comprehensive export of your digital session metadata, our Data Protection Officers are available to assist you during standard European business hours. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against unauthorized data sharing or invasive client tracking.

1. What Are Cookies and Digital Tokens?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit our digital atelier. They allow our website to recognize your device, maintain security protocols, store aesthetic preferences (such as light or dark theme selection), and analyze aggregate visitor engagement to refine our online experience.

At VelvetGarbQuay, we believe in radical digital minimalism. We deploy cookies strictly when necessary to ensure website functionality, preserve user visual preferences, and gather anonymized analytical telemetry. We never deploy aggressive cross-site tracking pixels or monetize browsing behavioral data.

2. Classification of Cookies We Deploy

VelvetGarbQuay deploys cookies across three strictly defined functional categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for foundational website navigation, security firewalls, and encrypted booking form submission. These cannot be disabled in our systems.
  • Aesthetic & Functional Preference Cookies: Remember your interface preferences, such as retaining your Light/Dark mode choice across browsing sessions via localStorage.
  • Analytical & Performance Cookies: Collect anonymous, aggregated telemetry regarding page load speeds, popular monograph reading metrics, and navigation paths via Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01). These cookies do not store personal identifying details.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can modify your cookie settings at any time through your web browser preferences. Most modern browsers allow you to block third-party cookies, delete existing stored cookies, or notify you when a cookie is placed. Please note that disabling strictly necessary functional cookies may impact navigation smoothness on our digital atelier.
