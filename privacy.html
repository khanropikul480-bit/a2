8. Detailed International Data Transfer Safeguards

Given the global reach of our haute couture operations across Europe, the United Kingdom, and North America, client records are routinely accessed across borders by our senior master cutters. To guarantee continuous protection of patron data, VelvetGarbQuay implements robust international data transfer mechanisms:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Legally binding data processing agreements approved by the European Commission that mandate strict encryption and administrative access limits on all international transfers.
  • UK Data Transfer Addendums: Ensuring seamless compliance with the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) framework for our London Mayfair atelier.
  • Zero-Trust Technical Access Architecture: Multi-factor authentication, granular role-based permissions, and end-to-end encrypted transport layer security (TLS 1.3) applied to all cross-border measurement queries.

9. Data Retention and Destruction Protocols

We retain client measurements and pattern drafting archives exclusively for the duration of your active relationship with our atelier. In the event that a patron requests account closure or pattern retirement, our compliance officers execute a certified data destruction protocol:

Physical paper patterns and muslin measurement notes are securely shredded using cross-cut industrial shredders, and all digital records across primary and backup databases are permanently overwritten using cryptographic erasure algorithms. Confirmation certificates of data destruction are provided to patrons upon formal request.

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality and Trust

At VelvetGarbQuay Haute Couture Atelier ("VelvetGarbQuay," "the Maison," "we," "our," or "us"), safeguarding the privacy and personal dignity of our discerning patrons is an essential cornerstone of our bespoke service. Whether you interact with our digital atelier online, visit our private salons in Paris, London, or New York, or commission custom garments, we handle all personal information with absolute confidentiality and rigorous administrative security.

This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines our standards and protocols regarding the collection, storage, utilization, and protection of personal identifiable information (PII), bespoke anatomical measurements, paper pattern archives, and communication records in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU 2016/679), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

We recognize that bespoke tailoring requires patrons to share sensitive physiological details regarding posture, body morphology, and personal lifestyle preferences. We treat this information with the highest standard of fiduciary discretion, maintaining strict internal controls to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

To deliver bespoke tailoring of peerless precision, we collect specific categories of information based on your voluntary engagement with our atelier:

  • Client Identification & Contact Information: Full legal name, formal salutation, telephone numbers, residential and delivery addresses, and private email contacts.
  • Anatomical Measurement Data & Postural Profiles: Precise 32-point biological measurements, shoulder incline slope measurements, spinal curvature records, and individual paper block drafting specifications necessary for custom pattern construction.
  • Commission Records & Garment Specifications: Historical records of commissioned garments, textile swatch references, custom dye formulas, fitting adjustment notes, and bespoke lining monogram details.
  • Digital Engagement & Browsing Information: IP addresses, browser types, session duration, device operating systems, and anonymous interaction analytics collected via strictly necessary and analytical cookies.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Payment verification details and billing transaction records processed through encrypted Level 1 PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers or banking credentials on our local servers.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Data Processing

We process personal data strictly under established lawful bases defined by international data protection frameworks:

  • Contractual Performance: Processing anatomical data, scheduling salon fittings, procuring bespoke textile yardage, and fulfilling physical delivery of finished couture garments.
  • Legitimate Interests: Managing archival pattern libraries to facilitate future bespoke orders, conducting internal quality assessments, and safeguarding the physical and digital infrastructure of our ateliers against fraud.
  • Explicit Consent: Distributing our seasonal Haute Gazette newsletter, private trunk show invitations, and monograph publications to patrons who have explicitly opted into digital communications.
  • Legal Obligation: Retaining financial transaction archives and tax documentation in compliance with French, British, and American commercial accounting laws.

4. Archival Storage and Physical Pattern Security

Individual client paper pattern blocks are maintained in secure, climate-controlled atelier vaults accessible exclusively by master cutters and designated client concierges. Digital measurement archives and bespoke records are stored on private, encrypted cloud databases protected by AES-256 bit encryption, multi-factor authentication, and continuous vulnerability monitoring.

We retain personal data and pattern archives for as long as a patron maintains an active commissioning relationship with the Maison. If a patron requests the archiving or destruction of their records, paper patterns are securely recycled and digital entries are permanently expunged from active databases within 30 business days, subject to mandatory statutory tax retention requirements.

5. Third-Party Disclosures & International Transfers

VelvetGarbQuay does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade client personal information under any circumstance. Information is disclosed solely to vetted third-party service partners under binding non-disclosure and data processing agreements:

  • Specialized insured fine-art courier services responsible for secure white-glove delivery of couture garments.
  • PCI-compliant financial processing platforms facilitating encrypted transaction settlements.
  • Accredited cloud infrastructure providers hosting encrypted digital communications and appointment booking portals.

When data is transferred across international jurisdictions (such as between our Paris, London, and New York ateliers), we ensure equivalent data protection levels via European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs).

6. Your Statutory Rights as a Patron

Under applicable global privacy regulations, you possess comprehensive statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You may request a complete copy of your personal data and anatomical measurement portfolio held by the Maison.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct any inaccuracies in your contact details or measurement records at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your client profile and digital measurement records.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may limit how we process your information or opt out of editorial dispatches with immediate effect.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your anatomical measurement specifications in a structured, machine-readable format.
